PiWire 2.2.0

GET STARTED
Open PiWire to follow the seven-step traffic-light introduction. Use Esc or
End tour at any step. Choose Settings > Start introduction to replay it.
On later starts, PiWire opens a new schematic with all 40 Raspberry Pi pins.

MACOS
Open the universal DMG and drag PiWire into Applications. Launch it there.
PiWire requires macOS 13 or later and supports Apple Silicon and Intel.
A double-click on a .piwire file opens the app assigned to it in Finder.
Finder can show the circuit thumbnail when Show icon preview is enabled.

WINDOWS
Extract the entire ZIP and open PiWire.exe. Keep the files together.
If it does not start, run Diagnose-PiWire.cmd in the extracted folder.
The log is saved to %TEMP%\PiWire-Start.log.

LINUX
Extract the TAR.GZ. From a graphical session, run ./start-piwire.sh as your
regular user. Electron requires the Chromium runtime libraries and sandbox
support for your distribution. To capture a startup log, run:

    ./start-piwire.sh --enable-logging > piwire-start.log 2>&1

DRAW AND SHARE
Choose components from the palette. Click two terminals to connect them.
Click the grid while drawing to place a corner. Select a wire to reveal its
corner handles. Click an existing wire to create a junction, or Shift-click
it to start from there. Component names, labels, and LED colors are editable
in Properties. Click the project title to rename the schematic.

Use File > Save As for an editable .piwire project, A4 PDF, or scalable SVG.
Choose Custom symbols to draw templates and share them as .piwire-symbol.
PDF and SVG exports use monochrome drawing lines.

EXAMPLES AND HELP
Open Beispiel-Ampel.piwire for the traffic light or Beispiel-LED.piwire for
a smaller circuit. PiWire Help includes the quick start and shortcuts.
